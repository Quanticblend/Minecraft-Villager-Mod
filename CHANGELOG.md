## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-15

### Added
- Stuck detection for villagers: open fence gates, hop one-block ledges, or detour around obstacles.
- Villagers walk to and open fence gates that block their route, then close them behind themselves.
- Village guards: right-click a villager with an iron sword to post it as a guard.
- Guards patrol their post, fight hostile mobs, regenerate, and can be moved or dismissed.
